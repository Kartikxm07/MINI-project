NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAPE is inflated by deep out-of-the-money options whose market price
  is near the $0.125 floor; a small $ error is a large % error there.
  MdAPE and RMSE give a more stable picture of typical accuracy.
- A single flat VIX volatility is used for every strike/maturity, so
  Black-Scholes cannot capture the volatility smile; systematic error
  across moneyness categories is expected and is the motivation for the
  downstream hybrid neural-network correction.
